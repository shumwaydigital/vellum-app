Common questions

Where are my memories kept?
Only on your iPhone. To keep a copy elsewhere, use You › Backup & restore.

Will I lose my memories when Vellum updates?
No. Updates, including TestFlight builds, keep everything you've made.

Can Vellum see my whole photo library?
Only if you allow it, and only on your iPhone. You can choose Limited Access in Settings › Apps › Vellum › Photos.
