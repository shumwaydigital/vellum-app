Privacy Policy

Vellum Memories · Effective October 23, 2026

Vellum is made to keep your family's memories private. In short: Vellum does not collect, sell or share any of your information. Your memories stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

The photos, videos, writing, names and settings you put in Vellum are stored only on your iPhone. The developer has no servers that receive them and cannot see them. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

Photos, camera and microphone

Vellum asks for access to your photo library so you can pick photos and so it can suggest memories worth keeping. It asks for the camera and microphone only when you take a photo or video in Vellum. Suggestions and titles are worked out on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device features; your pictures are not uploaded anywhere.

Places

If a photo has a location, Vellum can show the place's name. To find the name, the location is looked up with Apple's maps service, under Apple's privacy policy. Vellum does not track where you are.

When you choose to share

If you post a memory to Facebook, Instagram or another app, or export or back up your memories, the copy goes only where you send it, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Children

Vellum collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions are welcome on the Support page.
